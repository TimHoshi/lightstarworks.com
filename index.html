What I do

Old maps are online for free. The hard part is reading them right.

  • Knowing which old records really speak for your ground, and which only look like they do.
  • Lining the years up so the same field, mill, or road lands in the same spot on every map.
  • Catching the quiet things — an abandoned road, a building that vanished, a stream that used to run your property line.
  • Telling you plainly what you're looking at, and why it matters.
